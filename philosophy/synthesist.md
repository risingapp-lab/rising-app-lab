# Synthesist

Derived from the Greek word "Synthesis" (σύνθεσις),
meaning integration and combination.

A Synthesist integrates technologies,
people, experiences, and ideas to create new value.

Technology alone has no value.

Value emerges when technology,
people, and experiences are synthesized.

This is the essence of a Synthesist.

A Synthesist seeks value not in isolated technologies,
but in unexpected combinations.
